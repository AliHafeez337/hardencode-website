CPENT is a fully practical penetration testing exam. It is not a multiple choice quiz. The exam runs for 24 hours on a live range. You have to test systems, find a way in, prove the finding, and write the report. That is the whole point of the credential.

This page hosts a public copy of the certificate so a recruiter or client can open the file without a login wall. To confirm it with EC-Council, open aspen.eccouncil.org/Verify and enter serial ECC5927431086.

This is CPENT. It is not LPT Master. LPT Master is a higher score band on the same exam family, and that is not claimed here.
